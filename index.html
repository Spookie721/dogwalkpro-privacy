DogWalkPro

Privacy policy

Information stored on your device

DogWalkPro stores the information you enter to manage your dog-walking business. This can include business settings, dog profiles and photos, owner and emergency contact details, pickup addresses and coordinates, walk schedules, pickup progress, invoices and payment records. These records are stored in the app on your device. DogWalkPro has no account system, advertising, analytics or developer-operated server.

Pickup addresses and Apple Maps

When you search for an address, DogWalkPro sends the search text to Apple Maps to find the pickup point. When you request directions, DogWalkPro opens Apple Maps with the selected destination. Apple handles those requests under its own privacy practices. DogWalkPro does not record your live location or walk routes. You can use scheduling and invoicing without searching for pickup addresses.

Photos, exports and device services

DogWalkPro accesses photos you select through the iPhone photo picker. Photos used in dog profiles are saved locally. When you create a walk summary, the optional photo, notes, walk time, dog name and business name go into the PDF. The app creates the file locally and presents the iPhone share sheet. You choose whether and where to send or save it.

The same choice applies when you export invoices, CSV files or encrypted backups. The selected recipient or service handles the copy you share. Your iPhone may include app data in device backups according to your settings. For payment reversals, iOS handles Face ID or passcode verification and returns the authentication result to the app.

Retention and deletion

DogWalkPro keeps saved records on your device until you remove them or delete the app. Use the available controls in the app to remove individual records or a saved pickup address. You can remove an optional photo before sharing a summary. Exported files and device backups are managed separately in the apps or services where you saved them. The developer does not have access to your local records and cannot delete copies held by other recipients.

Contact

For privacy questions or help removing information, use the App Support link on the DogWalkPro App Store listing.
